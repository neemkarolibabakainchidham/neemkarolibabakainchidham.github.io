Verified Biography

Who Was Neem Karoli Baba?

Neem Karoli Baba (born Lakshmi Narayan Sharma, c. 1900, in Akbarpur village, Uttar Pradesh) was a revered Hindu saint whose life centred on bhakti — love, seva (selfless service), seeing the divine in every being, and constant remembrance of God. He is widely regarded by devotees as an avatar of Lord Hanuman.

During his lifetime he established ashrams at Kainchi and Vrindavan, and over 100 temples now stand in his name across India. The Kainchi Dham ashram, set in the scissor-shaped valley ("Kainchi" means "scissors" in Hindi) where the Kosi River flows past two intersecting hills, was inaugurated in June 1964 with a Hanuman temple, and became his primary residence in the last decade of his life.

Neem Karoli Baba left his body (attained mahasamadhi) on 11 September 1973 in Vrindavan. His teachings reached the West largely through American disciples such as Ram Dass (Richard Alpert), whose book Be Here Now introduced Maharaj-ji to a global audience.

Devotee Accounts

The Five Chamatkars (Miracles) of Neem Karoli Baba

These accounts are drawn from stories told and retold by devotees over decades — they form part of Kainchi Dham's oral and devotional tradition, not independently verified historical fact.

01

The Train Incident

Baba was once removed from a train for travelling without a ticket. Devotees say that once he stepped off, the train's engine refused to start again — and only resumed once he was allowed back aboard.

02

Endless Food from a Small Kitchen

At bhandaras (community feasts) held at Kainchi Dham, devotees recount that food prepared for a small gathering miraculously fed thousands, with supplies never appearing to run short.

03

Appearing in Two Places at Once

Multiple devotees have described Baba being seen or spoken to in two distant locations around the same time, which they attribute to his siddhis (spiritual powers).

04

Healing Through Presence and Blessing

Followers describe recoveries from serious illness and personal hardship that they credit to Baba's blessing, his glance, or simply sitting in his presence — often without any medicine involved.

05

Knowing the Unspoken

Many devotees, including Western visitors, describe Baba addressing their private thoughts, worries, or unspoken questions before they had said a word — an experience many call his most striking gift.

Verified Visitor History

Famous Visitors to Kainchi Dham

Kainchi Dham has drawn some of the world's most recognised figures from technology, business, and sport.

Apple Co-Founder
Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs travelled to India in 1974 as a young spiritual seeker and visited Neem Karoli Baba's ashram, an experience he later described as deeply influential on his outlook and on Apple's design philosophy.

Meta Founder & CEO
Mark Zuckerberg

Mark Zuckerberg visited Kainchi Dham, inspired partly by Steve Jobs' account of the ashram, and has spoken publicly about how the visit affected his thinking during a difficult period at Facebook.

Indian Cricket Icon
Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli and Anushka Sharma visited Kainchi Dham on 17 November 2022 with their daughter Vamika, offering prayers and chanting the Hanuman Chalisa. They have since visited Baba's Vrindavan ashram as well.

In Popular Culture

Hanuman Ansh (2026)

🎬
Hanuman Ansh
Official Poster
Released 7 Aug 2026 Biographical Drama Dir. Vishal Chaturvedi

Hanuman Ansh is a 2026 Hindi-language biographical devotional drama directly related to Neem Karoli Baba — it is the first instalment of a planned trilogy chronicling his life, from his early years as young Lakshmi Narayan in pre-Independence India to his transformation into the revered Maharaj-ji.

Made on a modest budget of around ₹2 crore, the film became a sleeper box-office success, going on to earn over ₹48 crore domestically — a testament to the continuing devotion Neem Karoli Baba inspires across India today.
